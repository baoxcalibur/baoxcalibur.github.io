Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Singularity collects, stores, and uses information required to provide its functionality.

Information Collected

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Guild (Server) IDs
  • Leveling data (XP, levels, message counts)
  • Guild configuration data
  • Moderation records and automoderation alerts

Message Content

Singularity does not store message content for leveling purposes. The Bot primarily stores message counts and moderation-related events required for functionality.

How Information Is Used

  • Leveling systems
  • Moderation systems
  • Guild configuration management
  • Feature operation and reliability

Data Storage

Data may be stored using MongoDB and Redis. Reasonable efforts are made to protect stored information.

Data Sharing

Singularity does not sell user data. Information is only used for Bot functionality.

Data Retention

Data may be retained for as long as necessary to provide functionality.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed through the Singularity support server.
